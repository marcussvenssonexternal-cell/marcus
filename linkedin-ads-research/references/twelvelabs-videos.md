# TwelveLabs LinkedIn video ads: reference notes

Three TwelveLabs video ads shared as references (Oct 2026), broken down frame by frame. The videos
themselves are not stored here. All three are 16:9 (1280×720), 25–30 seconds, with sound; the audio
could not be transcribed, but every video tells its story without it.

## What all three do

- **Open on the problem, shown rather than told.** The first 3–5 seconds make you feel the pain:
  an ad cutting into a car chase, or a cursor frantically scrubbing through footage.
- **One short line of text at a time**, black on white and centred, held for 2–3 seconds. About four lines in
  30 seconds in total.
- **Product as clean motion graphics, not screen recordings.** Rounded video cards, a filmstrip, a
  progress bar and labels in one accent colour (lime green), and a cursor that clicks things.
- **A concrete action as the payoff:** "+ ADD ADVERT" between SCENE END and SCENE START, or an
  "Export to social media" button turning green.
- **Plain-language labels** a person would use: "Rooftop confession", "Kitchen argument", "Weather".
- **Generic stock footage** (a police chase, a news studio), never recognisable films or shows.
- **Ending:** a payoff line, then 3–5 seconds of logo on a soft gradient. No CTA in the video; the CTA is
  LinkedIn's button.
- **Post copy:** a relatable pain line first, one line on the old way, one on what TwelveLabs does,
  three emoji bullets, and a short closing line (about 400 characters).

## What Vionlabs can take

1. **Hook with the felt problem.** Our Ad 3 video opens on a calm headline. An interruption or
   scrubbing opener would stop the scroll better.
2. **One idea and a few words per video.** Our videos show headline, UI, legend and CTA at once.
3. **Show a click that does something** (add the break, export the clip, add to a list), not only the analysis.
4. **Stock footage avoids the rights question** that the brief raises about real film stills.
5. **Where we go further:** TwelveLabs talks about scene boundaries and "natural pauses". Vionlabs can show
   the story itself (the emotional arc, with breaks in its valleys) and what rides on each break (mood, IAB
   category, brand safety). Our look stays distinct: dark navy and blue against their white and lime.
6. **Formats:** they run 16:9 only. The brief asks for 1:1 and 9:16, which suit LinkedIn mobile feeds better.
