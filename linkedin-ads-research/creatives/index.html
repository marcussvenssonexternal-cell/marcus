Vionlabs LinkedIn ad drafts

34 ads, grouped by the three audiences sales sells to, each with its own LinkedIn targeting. Built from what works in TwelveLabs’ current LinkedIn ads and made with real product screenshots and title art from vionlabs.com. Each card shows the image, the copy as LinkedIn shows it, and where every claim comes from.

What we took from TwelveLabs

  1. The intro text opens on the buyer’s problem, before naming the product.
  2. One big promise in the image headline, with the payoff in the accent colour.
  3. Product UI and simple diagrams instead of stock photos.
  4. Before → after layouts with ✕ and ✓ lists.
  5. Webinars co-branded with a bigger partner, with named speakers and a date.

Before any of these run

  • Have a source ready for every Check before launch claim. Most are on the website with no source behind them there (98%, 2×, 46%, 20+, 300M+).
  • Confirm Plex, Great American Pure Flix and i-CABLE are happy to be named in paid ads.
  • Check that the rights to the film and TV art (Skyfall, Gladiator, Peaky Blinders and others) cover paid ads, not only demos on the website.
  • Use TVBEurope’s official winner badge on the award ad.
  • Fill in the webinar date, speakers and partner (yellow in the image).

How to read a card

  • The label on each image and the first words of its post name the audience it’s for.
  • Grey text in the intro is hidden behind “…see more” on desktop (after about 150 characters).
  • Headline counts turn amber past 70 characters, where LinkedIn may cut them off.
  • Example values are mock-up numbers and names, not claims.
