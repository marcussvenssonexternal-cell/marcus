LinkedIn campaign · from Viktor’s ad brief · Oct 2026

AI built for entertainment

The campaign from Viktor’s ad brief, built in our own ad style with real title art and product screenshots from vionlabs.com. 11 ads: one brand ad, three audience ads, and seven feature follow-ups for the people who engage. Each ad has its images, carousels and videos, three headlines, three intro texts, targeting, and where every claim comes from. New at the top: a four-video series modelled on TwelveLabs’ video ads.

How the campaign runs

  1. Ad 0 (brand) runs first or in parallel, so people already know who Vionlabs is.
  2. Ads 1–3 each speak to one audience: AI and engineering, product and content ops, and media ops and automation.
  3. Follow-ups (Editorial Lab M1–M3, Creative Lab C1–C3, Operations Lab O1) retarget people who engaged with Ads 0–3.
  4. Video series (Videos 1–4) can run as follow-ups too, or as video versions of the feature ads for the same audiences.
  5. Every ad: “Book a demo”, at most 8 words on the image, a hook in the first 150 characters, and the sign-off “Vionlabs. AI built for entertainment.”

Before any of these run

  • On hold: film and TV imagery. The board flagged the film frames and title art (Succession, Gladiator, Troy and others) for rights confirmation before any paid delivery. 54 of 61 assets are affected, so every campaign stays paused. Picking smaller films lowers the risk of a complaint but doesn’t clear the rights: recognisable actors also need consent under Swedish law. The proposal is licensed stock and Creative Commons open films; see brief/rights-check.md.
  • Customer results (83%, 75%, 2x, 15%, 80%, and 278 days → 7 days for 65,000 episodes): confirm public use and how to credit them.
  • 90% library coverage (M2): no source found. Define what it measures.
  • 97% vs 92% retention with scene-matched breaks: left out until there’s a source.
  • “On-prem in your own cloud”: confirm the wording with the tech team.
  • Customer logos on Ad 0 card 5: needs the approved logo list (yellow placeholders).

How to read an ad

  • Switch between the three intro texts with 1 2 3. Grey text is hidden behind “…see more” on desktop (after about 150 characters).
  • Counts turn amber past 70 characters for headlines and 600 for intro texts.
  • From the brief facts are from Viktor’s brief. Example values are mock-up data, not claims.
  • Carousels scroll sideways. Videos play muted, as they do in the feed.
