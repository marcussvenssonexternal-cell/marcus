# Ten years of teaching machines to feel

_Founder story · for The whole industry, Partners and customers, Investors, Future hires_

**In 2016, AI that understands emotion in video sounded like science fiction. Ten years on, mood is a standard line on every metadata spec sheet. The interesting part is what Vionlabs got wrong on the way, and what Arash would tell a founder starting in media AI today.**

## Why now

The point of Arash's LinkedIn system is to make him a recognised voice in AI and streaming, and personal build stories travel further than product posts. 2026 is ten years since Vionlabs was founded, and the market has just caught up with the original bet: in the radar's first edition, five competitors were selling mood metadata.

## Questions the series answers

- What was the original bet in 2016, and who thought it was crazy?
- What did we build early that nobody wanted?
- Which change of direction mattered most, and how did we know?
- What is different about selling to broadcasters, streamers and telcos?
- What would Arash tell a founder starting a media-AI company in 2026?
