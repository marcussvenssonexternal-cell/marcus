# Choosing the 90 seconds

_Short-form, previews & artwork · for Marketing, editorial and promo teams, Small–mid streamers, Telco / cable operators_

**Every major streamer now has a vertical feed, and cropping 16:9 to 9:16 is a solved problem. Choosing which 90 seconds to crop is not, and that choice decides whether a clip turns into a full watch.**

## Why now

Netflix Clips, Disney+ Verts, HBO Max Shorts, Peacock and Tubi Scenes all launched feeds, and HBO Max still has people review the clips its AI picks. Peacock's 20% tap-through from highlights into live Olympics events is the only public conversion number, and it comes from sports. YouTube viewers watch more than 2 billion hours of Shorts on TV sets every month, and microdramas are projected at $3.6B outside China this year.

## Questions the series answers

- Sports has goals and red cards. What is the equivalent moment in a drama?
- Does a clip lead to a full watch, and who is measuring it?
- Previews, thumbnails and clips: one moment-picking problem with three outputs
- What microdramas can teach long-form about the first ten seconds
- When should a person still choose the clip?
