# Vionlabs brand brief (for the content radar)

The radar uses this brief to decide what is relevant and to write ideas in the right voice.
It is condensed from the Notion pages *New Joiner Hub*, *Sales Handbook → ICP*,
*Partnership Strategy* and *Arash Content Production*. Update it when positioning changes.

## What Vionlabs does

AI that understands *why* viewers watch what they watch, so every story finds its audience.
Emotion-aware, scene-level video understanding for streaming platforms, broadcasters and telcos.
Founded on the bet (2016) that AI could understand emotion in video.

| Product | One line |
| --- | --- |
| Metadata Enrichment | Mood, emotion and context per title and per scene: richer search, filtering, discovery |
| Custom Thumbnails | AI picks or generates artwork that matches the mood or key moments |
| Preview Clips & Shorts | Automatic previews and highlight clips (incl. vertical) from long-form |
| Intro & Credit Detection | Timestamps intros, recaps and end credits: skip features, segmentation |
| Ad-Break Creation | Finds natural narrative pauses where a break doesn't break the story |
| Contextual Ad-Breaks | Matches ad placement to scene mood and context |

Discovery experiences built on it: mood rows, *Because You Watched*, *Similar Titles*.

## Who we sell to (ICPs)

Small–mid streamers (niche content), small–mid streamers (regional), tier-1 streamers,
telco / cable operators, broadcasters, news broadcasters, ad-funded streamers (AVOD / FAST).

## Partners and proof points worth naming

AWS and Google Cloud (marketplaces), SDVI, Cinesys, Embrace / IVORY, Innotech (Hulu Japan),
SeaOvers (i-CABLE, live in 30 days), Plex, Zeam Media. Memberships and networks: DPP, IAMT.
Only name a customer in public content if it has already been announced publicly.

## Core theses (things we believe and can defend)

- The content libraries already exist. What's missing is the infrastructure to find the
  90 seconds inside a 90-minute film that makes someone stop scrolling. A discovery problem, not a content problem.
- Metadata tells you what is on screen. It never tells you why someone kept watching.
- Emotion and narrative structure are better signals than genre tags, for discovery *and* for ads.
- An ad break should land where the story pauses, not where the clock says.

### Voice

- Warm and crediting: name teammates and partners specifically
- Plain, short sentences. Genuine enthusiasm over restrained exec speak
- Curious, not preachy, even when making a firm point
- Specific over vague: real numbers, real names, real projects
- Acknowledge the other side before disagreeing. Confident, not combative
- First line is the opinion itself, not a lead-up. End on a specific question, not "thoughts?"
- Never: "in today's rapidly evolving landscape", "we're excited to announce",
  "it's important to note", or other generic corporate AI copy

Gut check: would this sound like Arash saying it to a friend at a conference bar?
